Privacy Policy

Store Sync POS Messenger for WhatsApp Web (browser extension)
Effective date: 16 October 2026

In short: the extension connects your Store Sync POS account to WhatsApp Web in your browser. It sends the customer messages your POS has queued, and it copies incoming customer messages back into your POS. The data goes only to your Store Sync POS server. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else. Store Sync POS Messenger is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by WhatsApp or Meta.

1. Who we are

This extension is published by OrdersHub ("we", "us"), the provider of the Store Sync POS. Contact us about this policy or your data at info@ordershub.pk.

The extension is a tool for businesses that use Store Sync POS. The business that installs it (for example, a shop) decides which customers are messaged and what is sent. We process this data on that business's behalf.

2. What the extension does

  • Sending: when you press Start, it fetches the next queued message (recipient phone number and message text) from your Store Sync POS, opens that chat in WhatsApp Web, and sends the message. It then tells your POS whether the message was sent or whether the number was unavailable.
  • Receiving: while listening is switched on, it reads new incoming messages in your WhatsApp Web chats and saves them to your Store Sync POS so your team can see and reply to them there. To read a chat, the extension opens it, which marks those messages as read in WhatsApp. The extension skips messages you sent yourself.
  • Sign-in: it reads your Store Sync POS sign-in from the POS website that is open in your browser, so you do not have to sign in again inside the extension.

The extension does not collect browsing history, read other websites, track you, show ads, or include analytics. It reads message text only. It does not read your contacts list, and it does not read or upload photos, voice notes, files, or WhatsApp call data.

4. Websites the extension accesses

  • https://web.whatsapp.com, to send and read messages as described above.
  • Your Store Sync POS website and its API, to read your sign-in and to exchange messages with your POS.

Data is sent over HTTPS to your Store Sync POS server and nowhere else.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade any data handled by the extension. We do not share it with third parties, except with service providers who host the Store Sync POS for us, or when the law requires it. Messages you send are delivered by WhatsApp under WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.

6. Chrome Web Store Limited Use

Our use of information obtained through this extension complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We use the data only to provide the features described above. We do not use it for advertising or credit decisions, and people do not read it except where you ask us to for support, where it is needed for security, or where the law requires it.

7. How long data is kept

  • In your browser: until you sign out of the POS, clear the extension's storage, or uninstall the extension.
  • On your Store Sync POS server: messages saved to your POS stay in your business's POS account until your business deletes them or closes its account, in line with your Store Sync POS agreement.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Turn off listening in the sidebar at any time to stop incoming messages from being saved.
  • Uninstall the extension to stop all processing and remove the data it keeps in your browser.
  • If you are a customer who messaged a business that uses Store Sync POS, ask that business first; it controls your messages. You can also contact us at info@ordershub.pk to access, correct, or delete your data, and we will work with the business to respond.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections, and only to your Store Sync POS server. Requests are authenticated with your POS sign-in token. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

10. Children

The extension is a business tool. It is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the extension handles data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. Significant changes will also be described in the extension's Chrome Web Store listing.
